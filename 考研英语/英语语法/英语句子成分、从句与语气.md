## 一、英语句法总览

### 1. 句子分析的三层框架
* **第一层：句子成分**（主语、谓语、宾语、补语、定语、状语、同位语等）
* **第二层：句子类型**（简单句、并列句、复合句）
* **第三层：语气系统**（陈述、疑问、祈使、感叹 + 虚拟语气）
---

## 二、句子的八大核心成分（考研语法分析基础）

### 1. 主语（Subject）
* **定义**：句子谈论的对象，通常在谓语前。
* **可充当主语的形式**：名词、代词、不定式、动名词、从句。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">The student</span> passed the exam.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Reading English newspapers</span> improves vocabulary.
3. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">To finish the project on time</span> is difficult.
4. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(What he said yesterday)</span> surprised everyone.

---

### 2. 谓语（Predicate）
* **定义**：说明主语“做什么/是什么/怎么样”，核心是谓语动词。
* **类型**：
  * 简单谓语：一个实义动词
  * 复合谓语：情态动词/助动词 + 动词原形/分词

**例句：**
1. She <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">studies</span> hard.
2. They <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">have been discussing</span> the plan.
3. You <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">should review</span> this chapter.

---

### 3. 宾语（Object）
* **定义**：动作的承受者，常位于及物动词后。
* **分类**：直接宾语、间接宾语、介词宾语、形式宾语。

**例句：**
1. He bought <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">a dictionary</span>.
2. She gave <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">me</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">a gift</span>.
3. We are talking about <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">the new policy</span>.
4. I find <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">it</span> important <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(to practice every day)</span>.

---

### 4. 表语（Predicative）
* **定义**：位于系动词后，说明主语的身份、性质、状态。
* **常见系动词**：be, become, seem, look, sound, remain, feel, grow, turn。

**例句：**
1. She is <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">a doctor</span>.
2. The soup tastes <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">delicious</span>.
3. The problem seems <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(much more complicated than we expected)</span>.

---

### 5. 宾语补足语（Object Complement）
* **定义**：补充说明宾语“是什么/变成什么/在做什么”。
* **常见结构**：
  * 宾语 + 名词/形容词
  * 宾语 + do/doing/done

**例句：**
1. We elected him <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">monitor</span>.
2. The news made me <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">happy</span>.
3. I saw her <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">cross the street</span>.
4. He kept the door <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">closed</span>.

---

### 6. 定语（Attribute）
* **定义**：修饰名词或代词，*就是中文里的形容词，后置定语就是放在被修饰词(名词)后面的形容词*，回答“什么样的、哪一个”。
* **位置**：前置定语（词）和后置定语（短语/从句）。

**例句：**
1. She bought <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">a new laptop</span>.
2. The book <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">on the table</span> is mine.
3. The man <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(who is speaking now)</span> is our professor.

---

### 7. 状语（Adverbial）
* **定义**：修饰*动词、形容词或全句*，表示时间、地点、原因、方式、条件、目的、结果、让步等，比如。
| 状语类型 | 中文举例 (状语在括号内) | 英语对应 | 逻辑功能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **时间状语** | 我 [早上八点] 起床。 | I get up [at 8:00 am]. | 何时发生？ |
| **地点状语** | 他 [在家里] 睡觉。 | He is sleeping [at home]. | 何地发生？ |
| **方式状语** | 请 [慢慢地] 说。 | Please speak [slowly]. | 怎么发生的？ |
| **程度状语** | 我 [非常] 喜欢你。 | I like you [very much]. | 程度深浅？ |
| **目的状语** | 他 [为了减肥] 跑步。 | He runs [to lose weight]. | 目的是什么？ |
| **原因状语** | [因为下雨]，我没去。 | I didn't go [because of the rain]. | 为什么发生？ |

**例句：**
1. We will meet <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">at 8 p.m.</span>.
2. She solved the problem <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">carefully</span>.
3. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Because it was raining)</span>, we stayed at home.
4. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If you work harder)</span>, you will improve quickly.

---

### 8. 同位语（Apposition）
* **定义**：对前面的名词进一步解释说明。

**例句：**
1. Mr. Wang, <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">our English teacher</span>, is very strict.
2. The fact <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that he failed again)</span> shocked his parents.

---

## 三、be 动词专题（你点名要求）

### 1. be 作系动词（最常见）
* 结构：主语 + be + 表语

**例句：**
1. The classroom <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">is</span> quiet.
2. My goal <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">is</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(to enter a top graduate school)</span>.

### 2. be 作助动词（进行时）
* 结构：be + doing

**例句：**
1. She <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">is preparing</span> for the exam.
2. They <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">were discussing</span> the report when I arrived.

### 3. be 作助动词（被动语态）
* 结构：be + done

**例句：**
1. The homework <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">was finished</span> on time.
2. More attention <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">should be paid</span> to detail.

### 4. There be 结构（存在句）
* 结构：There + be + 名词 + 地点/时间

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">There is</span> a book on the desk.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">There have been</span> many changes in recent years.

---

## 四、情态动词专题（你点名要求）

### 1. 核心情态动词与功能
* **can/could**：能力、许可、可能
* **may/might**：可能、许可
* **must**：必须、强烈推测
* **shall/should**：建议、义务
* **will/would**：意愿、习惯、委婉
* **need/dare**：需要、敢于（可作情态/实义）

### 2. 基本结构
* 情态动词 + 动词原形

**例句：**
1. You <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">must finish</span> your task today.
2. She <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">can speak</span> fluent English.
3. We <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">should review</span> this chapter again.

### 3. 情态动词完成式（考研翻译高频）
* **must have done**：过去一定做过（肯定推测）
* **may/might have done**：过去可能做过
* **could have done**：本可以做却没做
* **should/ought to have done**：本该做却没做
* **needn't have done**：本不必做却做了

**例句：**
1. He <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">must have forgotten</span> the meeting.
2. You <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">should have told</span> me earlier.
3. She <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">needn't have worried</span> so much.

---

## 五、句子类型：简单句、并列句、复合句

### 1. 简单句（Simple Sentence）
* 一个主谓结构，可带并列成分。

**例句：**
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">The students</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">are reviewing</span> in the library.

### 2. 并列句（Compound Sentence）
* 两个或以上分句，由并列连词连接（and, but, or, so, for, yet）。

**例句：**
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">I wanted to join the discussion</span>, but <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">I had no time</span>.

### 3. 复合句（Complex Sentence）
* 主句 + 一个或多个从句。

**例句：**
* I know <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that he will succeed)</span>.

---

## 六、名词性从句（主语/宾语/表语/同位语从句）

### 1. 主语从句（Subject Clause）
* 从句作主语，常可用 it 作形式主语。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(What he said at the meeting)</span> is true.
2. It is obvious <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that we need more practice)</span>.

### 2. 宾语从句（Object Clause）
* 从句作动词、介词或形容词后的宾语。

**例句：**
1. I believe <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that she can solve the problem)</span>.
2. We are worried about <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(whether he will arrive on time)</span>.

### 3. 表语从句（Predicative Clause）
* 从句位于系动词后作表语。

**例句：**
1. The fact is <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that we are running out of time)</span>.
2. My concern is <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(whether this method is reliable)</span>.

### 4. 同位语从句（Appositive Clause）
* 解释抽象名词的具体内容（fact, idea, news, belief, hope, suggestion 等）。

**例句：**
1. The news <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that our team won the match)</span> excited everyone.
2. We accepted the proposal <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that the meeting be postponed)</span>.

---

## 七、定语从句（Relative Clauses）

### 1. 限制性定语从句
* 不可省略，限定先行词范围。

**例句：**
1. The students <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(who work consistently)</span> usually improve faster.
2. This is the book <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that I mentioned yesterday)</span>.

### 2. 非限制性定语从句
* 可省略，补充说明，常用逗号隔开。

**例句：**
1. My father, <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(who is a doctor)</span>, works in Shanghai.
2. The report, <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(which was published last week)</span>, has drawn wide attention.

### 3. 关系代词与关系副词
* **关系代词**：who, whom, whose, which, that
* **关系副词**：when, where, why

**例句：**
1. This is the place <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(where we first met)</span>.
2. I remember the day <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(when she called me)</span>.
3. That is the reason <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(why he refused)</span>.

---

## 八、状语从句（Adverbial Clauses）全分类

### 1. 时间状语从句
* 连词：when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(When the lecture ended)</span>, we asked questions.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Before you make a decision)</span>, think carefully.

### 2. 原因状语从句
* 连词：because, since, as, now that

**例句：**
* We canceled the trip <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(because it was raining heavily)</span>.

### 3. 条件状语从句
* 连词：if, unless, provided that, as long as

**例句：**
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If you keep practicing)</span>, your writing will improve.

### 4. 让步状语从句
* 连词：although, though, even if, even though, while

**例句：**
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Although he was tired)</span>, he continued studying.

### 5. 目的状语从句
* 连词：so that, in order that

**例句：**
* We left early <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(so that we could avoid traffic)</span>.

### 6. 结果状语从句
* 连词：so...that..., such...that...

**例句：**
* It was <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">so difficult</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that many students gave up)</span>.

### 7. 比较状语从句
* 连词：than, as...as, not so/as...as

**例句：**
* She speaks English more fluently <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(than I do)</span>.

### 8. 方式状语从句
* 连词：as, as if, as though

**例句：**
* He talks <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(as if he knew everything)</span>.

## 九、非谓语结构（常作为句子成分的高级表达）

### 1. 不定式（to do）
* 可作主语、宾语、表语、定语、状语、补语。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">To master grammar</span> takes time.
2. I want <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to improve my translation skills</span>.
3. He came here <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to ask for help</span>.

### 2. 动名词（doing）
* 兼具动词与名词性质。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Reading aloud</span> helps pronunciation.
2. She is good at <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">organizing ideas</span>.

### 3. 分词（doing / done）
* 现在分词常表主动/进行，过去分词常表被动/完成。

**例句：**
1. The girl <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(standing by the window)</span> is my classmate.
2. The problem <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(discussed yesterday)</span> is still unsolved.

## 十、一致关系与倒装（补充你没提到但非常重要）

### 1. 主谓一致
* 语法一致、意义一致、就近一致。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Each student</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">has</span> a dictionary.
2. Not only the teacher but also the students <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">are</span> ready.

### 2. 倒装结构
* 否定副词前置、only 状语前置、so/such 前置、虚拟条件省 if。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Never have I seen</span> such a scene.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Only then did he realize</span> his mistake.
3. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Had I known</span> the truth, I would have acted differently.

## 十一、语气系统（重点：虚拟语气）

### 1. 陈述语气（Indicative Mood）
* 表示事实、判断、常规陈述。

**例句：**
* She <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">works</span> in a research institute.

### 2. 祈使语气（Imperative Mood）
* 表示命令、请求、建议。

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Please close the window</span>.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Do be careful</span> when crossing the road.

### 3. 虚拟语气（Subjunctive Mood）总览
* 表示与事实相反、可能性小、愿望、建议、命令、必要性等。

#### （1）if 条件句虚拟
* **与现在事实相反**：If + 过去式, would/could/might + do
* **与过去事实相反**：If + had done, would/could/might have done
* **与将来事实相反**：If + should do / were to do / did, would + do

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If I were you)</span>, I would study abroad.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If he had taken the advice)</span>, he would have succeeded.
3. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If it were to rain tomorrow)</span>, we would cancel the match.

#### （2）wish / if only 后虚拟
* wish + 过去式（对现在遗憾）
* wish + had done（对过去遗憾）
* wish + would do（对将来愿望/不满）

**例句：**
1. I wish <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(I knew the answer)</span>.
2. She wishes <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(she had not wasted so much time)</span>.
3. If only <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(it would stop raining)</span>.

#### （3）as if / as though 后虚拟

**例句：**
1. He speaks as if <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(he were an expert)</span>.
2. She looked as though <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(she had seen a ghost)</span>.

#### （4）would rather / would sooner / had rather

**例句：**
1. I'd rather <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(you came tomorrow)</span>.
2. He would rather <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(you had told him earlier)</span>.

#### （5）It is (high/about) time + 从句
* 从句常用过去式，表示“早该……”

**例句：**
* It is high time <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(we took action)</span>.

#### （6）表示“建议/命令/要求/必要”的名词性从句
* 常见触发词：suggest, demand, insist, order, request, propose；
  suggestion, proposal, order, demand；
  necessary, important, essential。
* 从句用：`(should) + 动词原形`（should 常省略）

**例句：**
1. The teacher suggested <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that we review the text again)</span>.
2. It is essential <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that every member be present)</span>.
3. Their proposal was <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that the project be postponed)</span>.

#### （7）含蓄虚拟（without, but for, otherwise）

**例句：**
1. Without your help, I <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">would have failed</span>.
2. But for the rain, we <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">would go</span> hiking.
3. He was busy; otherwise he <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">would have joined</span> us.

## 十二、从句与成分的“快判公式”（实战用）

### 1. 先找谓语，再切块
* 每个分句通常有自己的谓语动词。

### 2. 看到连接词先判从句类型
* that/whether/if（名词性从句）
* who/which/that/where/when（定语从句）
* because/if/although/when（状语从句）

### 3. 名词性从句四问法
* 这个从句在主句里充当什么？主语/宾语/表语/同位语？

### 4. 定语从句识别法
* 从句紧跟名词，且对该名词起修饰作用。

### 5. 虚拟语气识别法
* 看是否存在“与事实不符”的时间线或建议命令类触发词。

## 十三、综合分析示例（整句拆解）

### 例句 1
* The students <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(who have prepared carefully)</span> will perform better <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(when the final exam begins)</span>.

**拆解：**
1. 主干：The students will perform better.
2. `(who have prepared carefully)`：定语从句，修饰 students。
3. `(when the final exam begins)`：时间状语从句。

### 例句 2
* It is necessary <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that every participant submit the report on time)</span>.

**拆解：**
1. It 作形式主语。
2. 真主语是 `(that...)` 从句。
3. 从句谓语 submit 为虚拟（省略 should）。

### 例句 3
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If I had known the schedule)</span>, I would have arrived earlier.

**拆解：**
1. if 从句：过去完成时，表示与过去事实相反。
2. 主句：would have done，典型过去虚拟结果。

## 十四、五大基本句型（必须单独成图）

### 1. SV（主语 + 谓语）
* 例句：<span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">The children</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">laughed</span>.

### 2. SVC（主语 + 系动词 + 表语）
* 例句：<span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">The plan</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">is</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">feasible</span>.

### 3. SVO（主语 + 谓语 + 宾语）
* 例句：<span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">We</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">completed</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">the task</span>.

### 4. SVOO（主语 + 谓语 + 间宾 + 直宾）
* 例句：<span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">She</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">sent</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">me</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">an email</span>.

### 5. SVOC（主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 + 宾补）
* 例句：<span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">They</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">found</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">the lecture</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">helpful</span>.

## 十五、省略、替代与强调（长难句常考）

### 1. 省略（Ellipsis）
* 状语从句省略：
  - 原句：When <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(she was)</span> in college, she published papers.
  - 省略后：When <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">in college</span>, she published papers.
* 不定式省略：
  - I wanted to help, but I had no time <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(to help)</span>.

### 2. 替代（Substitution）
* do/does/did 替代前文动作：
  - She studies harder than I <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">do</span>.
* so/not 替代从句：
  - “Will he come?” “I think <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">so</span>.”

### 3. 强调句（Cleft Sentence）
* 结构：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分
* 例句：It was <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">in 2024</span> <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that she started this project)</span>.

---

## 十六、定语从句进阶细则（高频陷阱）

### 1. that 不能用于非限制性定语从句
* 误：My father, that works in Beijing, ...
* 正：My father, <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(who works in Beijing)</span>, ...

### 2. 介词 + which/whom
* The company <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(for which he works)</span> is expanding.
* The professor <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(to whom I wrote)</span> replied quickly.

### 3. as 引导非限制性定语从句
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(As we all know)</span>, practice makes perfect.
* The report was delayed, <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(as was expected)</span>.

---

## 十七、名词性从句易错点（what/that, whether/if）

### 1. what vs that
* **that** 在从句中不充当成分，仅起连接作用。
* **what** 在从句中充当成分（主语/宾语/表语）。

**例句：**
1. I know <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that he is honest)</span>.
2. I know <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(what he needs)</span>.

### 2. whether vs if
* 介词后通常用 whether：
  - We are concerned about <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(whether the policy will change)</span>.
* 与 or not 连用优先 whether：
  - I don’t know <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(whether or not he will attend)</span>.
* 主语从句/表语从句更稳用 whether：
  - <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Whether he agrees)</span> is still uncertain.

---

## 十八、虚拟语气高级考点补充

### 1. 混合虚拟（时间错位）
* 条件从句说过去，主句说现在结果：
  - <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(If I had taken your advice)</span>, I <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">would be</span> much better now.

### 2. suggest/insist 的双义
* suggest 表“建议”时，从句常用虚拟（should）do：
  - She suggested <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that we start earlier)</span>.
* insist 表“坚持要求”时可虚拟；表“坚持认为”时常用陈述语气：
  - He insisted <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that we be punctual)</span>.（要求）
  - He insisted <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(that he was right)</span>.（认为）

### 3. 倒装虚拟再补三型
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Were I in your position)</span>, I would accept the offer.
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Should you need help)</span>, just call me.
* <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">(Had she prepared better)</span>, she would have passed.

---

## 十九、非谓语的时态与语态（和句子成分联动）

### 1. 不定式系统
* to do（一般主动）
* to be done（一般被动）
* to have done（完成主动）
* to have been done（完成被动）

**例句：**
1. She hopes <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to publish</span> soon.
2. The book is difficult <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to understand</span>.
3. He seems <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to have forgotten</span> the date.
4. The bridge is believed <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">to have been built</span> in 1890.

### 2. 分词系统
* doing（主动/进行）
* done（被动/完成）
* having done（先于谓语）
* being done / having been done（被动过程/先被动后）

**例句：**
1. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Having finished the report</span>, she went home.
2. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Being invited to speak</span>, he felt honored.
3. <span style="text-decoration: underline wavy;">Having been warned</span>, they became more careful.
